# Fuse Energy usage from its private API: projects compared, and the protocol (17 Oct 2026)

**Why:** Bright (DCC data) is often hours or a day behind, and gives no live readings. The household is on Fuse Energy (fixed tariff, electricity and gas). The user asked for community projects that use a Fuse account login to read usage, so we can copy the best one's approach.

**How:** two research agents worked in parallel.
- One surveyed and ranked every public project, reading GitHub pages and raw files.
- The other read the three main repos at their current commits, line by line, to pin down the protocol.

The proxy here refused both `api.fuseenergy.com` and `www.fuseenergy.com` (CONNECT 403), so nothing could be tested against Fuse itself. We did not route around that. community.home-assistant.io, MoneySavingExpert and Reddit were also blocked, so claims from those sites rest on search snippets only.

Earlier Fuse notes from 13 Oct are in [2026-10-13-usage-after-octopus.md](2026-10-13-usage-after-octopus.md). This note corrects them where they differ (for example, `x-fuse-app-version` isn't enforced for the SMS step).

## Conclusion

- **Copy the approach of [yakupafsin/fuse-energy-ha](https://github.com/yakupafsin/fuse-energy-ha) (MIT, v0.2.2, 21 Sep 2026).**
  - It is the most complete: every supply (electricity import and export, gas) and accurate cost. It handles the clock change, the hour in progress, Fuse's SMS refusals and rate limit, the identity questions, and token rotation.
  - It is the most recent, and has about 100 test checks plus CI.
  - It is the only project with a real user confirming it works ("I'm in! It worked on 0.1.3", [issue #6](https://github.com/yakupafsin/fuse-energy-ha/issues/6), 16 Sep 2026).
  - No breakage has been reported since. That is an absence of reports, not proof.
- **Decision, 17 Oct (later the same day):** the user chose to license the panel **AGPL-3.0-only**. Code can then be ported from both simonsolts (AGPL-3.0-only) and yakupafsin (MIT), keeping their notices (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `LICENSES/` in the repo root), and the dispute below no longer matters for us. Superseded advice, kept for the record:
- **No code is copied: only protocol facts.** [Issue #13](https://github.com/yakupafsin/fuse-energy-ha/issues/13) is open. In it, simonsolts says parts of yakupafsin's code derive from his AGPL project; yakupafsin added credit but declined to relicense. Reimplementing from the facts below avoids that dispute. A courtesy credit goes in the docs. This is not legal advice.
- **Nothing gives live watts.**
  - Every 2026 project reads one endpoint, which gives **hourly** bars per supply per UK day.
  - The hour in progress is marked REALISED and its value climbs. Measured on 17 Sep, it updated at half past the hour: 0 kWh until 00:29, then 0.125 kWh from 00:30:59. So "today so far" lags at most about 30–60 minutes.
  - Compare Bright, often a day behind.
- **A browser page will very likely need a relay.**
  - Every client is server-side: Home Assistant, the JVM, or the Android app.
  - Fuse's own web dashboard is a Next.js app. It calls only its own `www.fuseenergy.com/api/trpc/…` with cookies, never `api.fuseenergy.com` from the browser.
  - Every call needs a CORS preflight: `Authorization`, `Device-Id`, `x-fuse-app-version` and a JSON body all trigger one.
  - No `Access-Control-*` evidence exists anywhere. [unverified: test from the tablet]

## The protocol, for a JavaScript reimplementation

Line references are to yakupafsin @ `48c1e6d` (**Y**) and simonsolts @ `962bfed` (**S**), under `custom_components/fuse_energy/`.

### Device-Id
- A random lowercase v4 UUID (`crypto.randomUUID()`).
- Make it once, keep it with the tokens, and reuse it on every call, refresh and new sign-in (Y config_flow.py:90-92, 324-326).
- Y says Fuse ties sessions to it, and that a new one can trigger extra identity checks [unverified]. So keep it even across sign-out.

### Headers
- On `api.fuseenergy.com`:
  - `Content-Type: application/json`
  - `Accept: application/json`
  - `Device-Id: <uuid>`
  - `Authorization: Bearer …` where needed.
- No custom User-Agent is needed: HA's default is accepted (Y api.py:106-113).
- The 2024 Android app sent more headers (`Session-Id`, `X-Request-Id`, `Device-Model`, `Time-Zone: Europe/London`). The 2026 clients don't need them, although andrejkurlovic says `Time-Zone` was required on its email flow.

### Sign-in (four calls)

**1. Start the flow.** This mints a flow token and sends no SMS (Y auth.py:127-161, 282-312).
```
POST https://api.fuseenergy.com/api/v3/auth
Content-Type: application/json
Device-Id: <uuid>
{"challenge_type":"INITIAL","data":{"method":"PHONE","data":{"phone_number":"+447700900123","prelude_dispatch_id":null}}}
→ 2xx {"auth_flow_token":"<JWT>","challenge_type":"PHONE_OTP","data":{…}}
→ 4xx {"status_string":"<reason>"}      5xx: transient, try later
```
The flow token is short-lived (minutes).

**2. Send the SMS.** This goes to the website's tRPC route and is what actually sends the code (Y auth.py:314-348).
```
POST https://www.fuseenergy.com/api/trpc/phoneSignIn
Content-Type: application/json
x-fuse-app-version: 0.5.388
{"phone":"+447700900123"}
```
- Plain JSON: no `?batch=1`, no `{"0":…}`, no `{"json":…}` wrapper. No cookies, Device-Id or Authorization.
- The key must be `phone`. With `phoneNumber` it returns HTTP 500. The forms `+447…`, `447…` and `07…` all behaved the same (Y commit a6ecbd3).
- **Refusals come back as HTTP 200 with the reason in the body.** Captured: `{"result":{"data":{"error":{"errorCode":"incorrect_phone_number"}}}}`.
- The rate limit's code is `issue_otp_premature_retry`; its envelope wasn't captured. Search the whole body for an `errorCode`, `error.code`, `code` or `message`, as Y auth.py:184-228 does.
- Treat a premature retry as "wait a few minutes, don't retry". In issue #6, about 15 minutes wasn't always enough.
- The success body was never captured: treat 200 with no error as sent.
- `x-fuse-app-version` isn't enforced here: "5.314 and a made-up 9.999 behave identically" (Y PR #7). Keep the value in one constant.

**3. Submit the code.** (Y auth.py:231-267, 350-382)
```
POST https://api.fuseenergy.com/api/v3/auth
Content-Type: application/json
Device-Id: <same>
Authorization: Bearer <auth_flow_token>
{"challenge_type":"PHONE_OTP","data":{"code":"123456"}}
→ {"challenge_type":"AUTHORIZED","data":{"access_token":"<JWT>","refresh_token":"<opaque>"}}
→ {"challenge_type":"ADDITIONAL_INFO","auth_flow_token":"<NEW JWT>","data":{"title":"Verify your date of birth","subtitle":"…","questions":[{"key":"DATE_OF_BIRTH","title":"Date of birth","type":"DATE"}]}}
→ 4xx {"status_string":"invalid_code"}
```
Always keep the **newest** `auth_flow_token`.

**4. Identity questions.** These come only when asked, and can repeat (Y auth.py:356-364; S config_flow.py:228-233).
```
POST https://api.fuseenergy.com/api/v3/auth     (Bearer = the newest flow token)
{"challenge_type":"ADDITIONAL_INFO","data":{"responses":{"DATE_OF_BIRTH":"1965-03-04"}}}
```
- `DATE` answers are `YYYY-MM-DD`; anything else is text.
- A postcode question is mentioned only in comments; its key is unknown, so render whatever `questions[]` says.

### Refresh (Y auth.py:385-432)
```
POST https://api.fuseenergy.com/api/v1/auth/refresh
Content-Type: application/json
Device-Id: <same>
Authorization: Bearer <current access token, even expired>
{"refresh_token":"<current>","original_request_path":null}
→ 2xx {"access_token":"…","refresh_token":"…"}      (top level, not under data)
→ 4xx {"status_string":…}: the session is over, sign in again (a new SMS)
```
- Sending only the refresh token is rejected.
- **Both tokens rotate, and the old refresh token dies at once.** Save the new pair before using it.
- Refresh in one place only:
  - One lock (Web Locks across tabs).
  - After getting the lock, re-read storage in case another tab already refreshed.
  - Then retry the call once.
- Only one browser or app may hold the session: the kiosk app and Chrome would invalidate each other.
- The access token lasts 15 minutes (Y PR #12). The token response has no expiry field; using the JWT's `exp` claim is an inference.
- Refresh-token lifetime and how often a new SMS is needed: unknown.

### Data
```
GET https://api.fuseenergy.com/api/v2/customer/premises
→ [{"premises":{"id":"<uuid>", …}, "supplies":[…], "default_date_uk":"2026-05-25"}]

GET https://api.fuseenergy.com/api/v1/premises/{premisesId}/chart?year=2026&month=10&day=17   (one UK-local day)
→ {"current_index":{"year":2026,"month":10,"day":17,"hour":14},
   "supplies":[{"supply_fid":"…","supply_type":"ELEC_IMPORT",
     "bars":[{"bar":{"index":{"year":2026,"month":10,"day":17,"hour":0},
                     "kWh":"0.422","money":{"amount":"0.12","currency":"GBP"},"type":"REALISED"},
              "breakdown":[{"name":"USAGE","value":{"amount":"0.1086"},"kWh":"0.422"},
                           {"name":"STANDING","value":{"amount":"0.0181"},"kWh":null}]}, …]},
     {"supply_type":"ELEC_EXPORT",…}, {"supply_type":"GAS",…}]}
```
Assembled from fixtures and PR excerpts, not a raw capture (Y api.py:160-347; S tests/test_api.py:86-140; Y PR #8).

How to read it:
- **Units:**
  - **kWh** is a string: `Number(bar.kWh ?? bar.kwh)`.
  - **Cost** is the sum of every `breakdown[].value.amount` (pounds, 4 decimals). Fall back to `bar.money.amount` only when there is no breakdown. The 2-decimal field is truncated: it matched truncation on 48 of 48 bars, and summing it read about 10% low, while the breakdown sums matched the app exactly (Y commit 840aa13).
  - **Standing charge** is a `STANDING` component on every hour. In the sample, 0.0181 £/h is about 43.4p a day.
- **Which bars count:**
  - **REALISED** bars only. Later hours are `FORECASTED`.
  - **The hour in progress** is REALISED with a climbing value, and updates at :30. Count it in today so far, and mark it "so far".
  - **Revisions:** recent hours keep changing for some hours. Re-read today on every poll, and yesterday now and then.
  - **Other days:** drop bars whose index date isn't the day asked for, since padding can appear (Y api.py:199-203).
- **Time:**
  - **Time zone:** bars are indexed by Europe/London calendar hour.
  - **October clock change:** Y assumes hour 1 appears twice and maps the second to GMT (synthetic test only). A real clock-change payload has never been captured. Spring forward (23 bars?) is unknown.
- **Supplies:**
  - **Export:** whether its money is signed is unknown. The panel doesn't need export.
  - **Gas:** Y reads hourly gas bars. andrejkurlovic saw gas arrive daily, about a day behind. Expect gas to lag electricity: the meter's battery limits how often it reports.

### Polling and limits
- **Polling:** both HA projects poll every 15 minutes. One chart call for today every 15 minutes, plus yesterday occasionally, is enough. Don't loop through many past days: unbounded catch-up is an open question (Y issue #10).
- **Errors:** the only SMS limit seen is `issue_otp_premature_retry`. Handle 426 ("app version too old") and 429 (with Retry-After) as plain-words errors.
- **Blocking:** no reports of anyone being blocked.

### CORS: how to test (from the tablet, or a normal computer)
```
curl -si -X OPTIONS https://api.fuseenergy.com/api/v2/customer/premises \
  -H 'Origin: https://anginsemilir.github.io' -H 'Access-Control-Request-Method: GET' \
  -H 'Access-Control-Request-Headers: authorization,device-id'
curl -si -X OPTIONS https://www.fuseenergy.com/api/trpc/phoneSignIn \
  -H 'Origin: https://anginsemilir.github.io' -H 'Access-Control-Request-Method: POST' \
  -H 'Access-Control-Request-Headers: content-type,x-fuse-app-version'
```
If neither answers with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` for the panel's origin, a relay is needed, as for Bright.

## Seen on a real account (18 October 2026, from the panel's "Show what Fuse sent")

- **The month and year charts answer as assumed.** `/chart?year&month` returns one bar per day with `index` `{year, month, day}`; `/chart?year` one bar per month with `index` `{year, month}`; `current_index` stops at the day or month. `kWh` is a string, and the same `breakdown` (USAGE, STANDING) is there. Only the days and months from when the account began are present (9 October here), and the rest of the year up to December comes as `FORECASTED` bars, which the panel ignores.
- **A realised bar can have no usage:** the first day's electricity was `0` kWh with only a STANDING part (£0.34), and gas had `0` on the 11th and 18th and `1.071` on the 12th. The gas meter reports irregularly, so days are filled in late.
- **Hourly gas is not hourly.** Yesterday's 24 gas bars were all `0.394` kWh, and 24 × 0.394 = 9.456, the daily figure the month gives for that day (`9.465`). So Fuse shares the day's gas total evenly over 24 bars. Today's gas bars up to the current hour were `0.428` each and labelled REALISED, while the month view had `0` for today: an estimate. The panel therefore treats gas as once a day when eight or more finished hours are identical (today's or yesterday's), drops the hourly gas graph, and doesn't count today's gas.
- Per supply, the chart also has `realised_money`, `realised_usage_kWh` and `total_money`, and the whole chart `total_realised_money`. The panel adds up the bars instead; these may be a cross-check, but their values weren't printed.
- Rates seen: electricity 24.7p/kWh and 34.8p/day standing; gas 8.5p/kWh and 11.8p/day standing.
