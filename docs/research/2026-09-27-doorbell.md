# Nest doorbell: full-screen live view and chime on the wall panel

Prepared 27 Sep 2026 for https://anginsemilir.github.io/Home-Dashboard-/ on a Lenovo Tab M10 (TB328FU, Android 12). Each claim has its source in brackets. **[Unsure]** marks a step that could not be checked against a live screen or account.

## Four things to know first

1. **A press only reaches the panel through Pub/Sub.** Google delivers doorbell presses only as `sdm.devices.events.DoorbellChime.Chime` messages on Cloud Pub/Sub. The doorbell trait has no fields or commands, so there is nothing the panel can check on a timer. [https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/traits/device/doorbell-chime (search snippet); https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/subscribe-to-events (snippet)] The owner creates a topic and a subscription in their own Google Cloud project, and the panel reads from it directly in the browser. That works from the panel's address: on 27 Sep 2026 `pubsub.googleapis.com` accepted cross-site requests from `https://anginsemilir.github.io`, allowing `authorization,content-type` headers and POST/PUT.
2. **Expected cost is £0, but billing must stay on.** Pub/Sub includes 10 GiB free per month [https://cloud.google.com/pubsub/pricing; https://cloud.google.com/free]. It still needs a billing account, and the free trial "auto-closes if you spend the $300 credit or 90 days pass". After that, "your workloads get shut down" and are deleted after a 30-day grace period [https://cloud.google.com/signup-faqs]. The owner must click **Upgrade** before the trial ends.
3. **It only works while the panel is the app on screen.** A web page cannot turn the screen on or bring itself to the front [https://developer.android.com/guide/components/activities/background-starts; https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Screen_Wake_Lock_API].
4. **Check the doorbell model.** The Nest Doorbell (wired, 3rd gen, 2025) does not send press events at all. It is a Google bug, open since Dec 2025 and reset to "New" in July 2026 [https://github.com/home-assistant/core/issues/177196; https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/468317741]. The battery doorbell and the wired 1st and 2nd gen are not reported as affected.

With Google Home Premium, the alert text may be an AI description ("Person walking with a dog") [https://9to5google.com/2025/10/01/gemini-for-home-app-launch/; https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/01/google-updates-its-home-app-with-gemini-smarts/].

# A. OWNER INSTRUCTIONS

## Part 1: Works today, no panel change (Google Home alerts on the tablet)

**What it does:** when someone presses the doorbell, a banner slides down over the panel with a sound. Tap it to open the doorbell's live view in the Google Home app, where you can also use Quick Responses and Talk [https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/9225663].

**What it can't do:**
- **No full-screen, phone-call-style alert.** Google only offers that on a Pixel Tablet in Hub Mode [https://support.google.com/googlepixeltablet/answer/13680453].
- **Tapping it leaves the panel.** It opens the Google Home app, not the panel. Press Back to return.
- **Screen off:** sound only, no banner.
- **Pinned screen:** if WebView Kiosk's **Lock** (screen pinning) is on, Android hides and silences other apps' alerts [AOSP Android 12 LockTaskController.java / NotificationManagerService.java].
- **Do Not Disturb:** blocks the alert unless you let it override Do Not Disturb (step 6) [AOSP android12 default_zen_mode_config.xml].

Steps:
1. On the tablet, install **Google Home** from the Play Store. Sign in with a Google account that is a member of your home.
2. Open Google Home → **Home** tab → **Devices** (or **Cameras**) → tap the doorbell → **Settings** (gear) → **Notifications**. **[Unsure: menu names moved in the Oct 2025 redesign.]**
3. Turn **Push notifications** on, and make sure the doorbell ring alert is on. Turn **Away-only** off.
   - These settings are per account, so they affect your phone too.
   - Keep them on even if you later use Part 2: they also decide what Google sends to the panel ([https://raw.githubusercontent.com/home-assistant/home-assistant.io/current/source/_integrations/nest.markdown], "Google Home App Notification Settings").
4. Tablet **Settings → Apps → See all apps → Home → Notifications**: allow notifications.
5. Open the doorbell category, which Nest users report is called **Doorbell press notifications** (**[Unsure]** of the exact name). Set:
   - **Alerting** (not Silent)
   - **Pop on screen**: on
   - **Sound**: pick a ringtone
   [https://support.google.com/android/answer/9079661]
6. Optional: in the same place, turn on **Override Do Not Disturb**.
7. Keep the screen on while charging: **Settings → About tablet** → tap **Build number** 7 times → **Settings → System → Developer options → Stay awake** → on [https://developer.android.com/studio/debug/dev-options].
8. In WebView Kiosk, **don't use Lock** if you want these alerts.
9. Test by pressing the doorbell.

Optional, **[Unsure, not tested on this tablet]**: the free MacroDroid app can bring the panel back to the front when the Google Home alert arrives.

- Actions: **Screen On**, then **Launch Application → WebView Kiosk**. MacroDroid needs "Display over other apps" permission.
- Match on your doorbell's name rather than a fixed word, because Gemini may reword the alert.

## B2. OAuth scope (google.js)
```js

// authUrl(): ask for it only when a doorbell subscription is configured
const scopes = [g.projectId ? SCOPE_SDM : null, g.projectId && g.doorbellSub ? SCOPE_PUBSUB : null, SCOPE_CAL, SCOPE_TASKS].filter(Boolean).join(' ');

// api(url, opts = {}, timeoutMs) → pass timeoutMs through as fetchJSON's 3rd argument
```
- **The request itself:** same Partner Connections URL, with `access_type=offline` and `prompt=consent`. Home Assistant requests exactly `sdm.service` plus `pubsub` through the same URL [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/home-assistant/core/dev/homeassistant/components/nest/const.py; test_config_flow.py].
- **Why this scope:** pull and acknowledge accept only `auth/pubsub` or `auth/cloud-platform`, and `auth/pubsub` is the narrower of the two [discovery doc].
- **Existing test:** `redirectUri and authUrl` in `google-nest.test.js` stays valid, because `doorbellSub` is absent there.
- **Security caveat, for `docs/google.md`:** the `pubsub` scope lets the token "View and manage Pub/Sub topics and subscriptions" anywhere the account has rights, and the refresh token sits in localStorage on the shared github.io origin (already noted in `docs/research/2026-09-25-review-web-app.md`). There is no narrower scope. Asking for it only when a subscription is configured keeps the exposure opt-in.

## B4. Endpoints
All requests go to `PS = 'https://pubsub.googleapis.com/v1'` with `Authorization: Bearer <token>` and `Content-Type: application/json`. The cross-site preflight is allowed from the github.io origin and cached for 3600 s (probed live 27 Sep 2026).

| Purpose | Request | Body | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pull | `POST ${PS}/${sub}:pull` | `{"maxMessages":10}`. `maxMessages` is required; never send `returnImmediately`, which is deprecated [discovery doc]. | `{}`, or `{"receivedMessages":[{"ackId","message":{"data":<base64>,"messageId","publishTime"}}]}` |
| Ack | `POST ${PS}/${sub}:acknowledge` | `{"ackIds":[…]}` | `{}` |
| One-time event trigger | `GET https://smartdevicemanagement.googleapis.com/v1/enterprises/{projectId}/devices`, i.e. the existing `Nest.devices()` | none | device list |

- **Why the device list:** Device Access starts publishing only after a `devices.list` call made with the new token [https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/subscribe-to-events (snippet)]. The listener calls it once per page load. At the moment `main.js` only calls `devices.get`.
- **Precedent:** Google's own sample web app pulls and acks from the browser [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/device-access-sample-web-app/master/public/events.js]. It authenticates with a service-account key, which a public site must not do; the panel uses the user token instead.

## B5. Pull loop (doorbell.js)
```js
export const STALE_MS = 60e3, PULL_TIMEOUT_MS = 120e3, MIN_CYCLE_MS = 2e3, LOOPS = 2,
  BACKOFF_START_MS = 2e3, BACKOFF_MAX_MS = 60e3, CONFIG_RETRY_MS = 10 * 60e3, OFFLINE_AFTER_MS = 2 * 60e3, SEEN_KEEP_MS = 15 * 60e3;

async #loop(gen) {
  let backoff = 0;
  while (gen === this.gen) {
    const t0 = this.now();
    let r;
    try {
      r = await this.g.api(`${PS}/${this.sub}:pull`, { method: 'POST', headers: JSON_H, body: '{"maxMessages":10}', signal: this.ctrl.signal }, PULL_TIMEOUT_MS);
    } catch (e) {
      if (gen !== this.gen) return;                      // stopped
      if (e?.name === 'AbortError') continue;            // our 120 s timeout: pull again at once
      const config = isConfigError(e);                   // HttpError 400/401/403/404, or "sign in again"
      this.#fail(explainDoorbellError(e, this.sub), config);
      backoff = config ? CONFIG_RETRY_MS : Math.min(BACKOFF_MAX_MS, backoff ? backoff * 2 : BACKOFF_START_MS);
      await this.sleep(backoff, this.ctrl.signal); if (config) backoff = 0;
      continue;
    }
    if (gen !== this.gen) return;   // arrived after stop(): don't handle or ack; Pub/Sub redelivers after the 10 s ack deadline
    backoff = 0; this.#ok();
    const got = r?.receivedMessages || [];
    if (got.length) {
      for (const m of got) this.#handle(m);              // ring first (synchronous start of the UI)…
      this.g.api(`${PS}/${this.sub}:acknowledge`, { method: 'POST', headers: JSON_H,
        body: JSON.stringify({ ackIds: got.map((m) => m.ackId) }) }).catch(() => {});   // …then ack EVERY message
    } else if (this.now() - t0 < MIN_CYCLE_MS) await this.sleep(MIN_CYCLE_MS - (this.now() - t0), this.ctrl.signal);
  }
}
```
- **Why 120 s:** an empty REST pull can be held open for about 90 s [https://github.com/googleapis/google-cloud-php/issues/710]. If the browser gives up before the server does, messages already reserved for that pull arrive late [https://github.com/noetl/tools/pull/100].
  - Google's own clients use 60 s [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/googleapis/googleapis/master/google/pubsub/v1/pubsub_grpc_service_config.json].
  - `x-server-timeout` passes the preflight check, but it is untested for Pub/Sub; leave it out of v1.
- **Why 2 loops, started 1 s apart:** Google advises keeping several unary pulls outstanding for low latency [https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/pull-troubleshooting (snippet)]. A reply of `{}` doesn't mean the queue is empty.
- **Cost bound:** the 2 s floor caps billed requests at 1 per second. That's about 2.59 M requests at the 1 KB minimum, roughly 2.4 GiB a month, under the 10 GiB free allowance [https://cloud.google.com/pubsub/pricing: "A minimum of 1 KB is assessed for each request"].
- **Backoff:** Home Assistant uses 10 s ×1.5 up to 10 min [google-nest-sdm streaming_manager.py]. The panel uses 2 s doubling to 60 s, because a doorbell needs faster recovery and the cost is trivial.
  - Configuration errors (400/401/403/404) wait 10 min instead of backing off, and the listener restarts immediately when Settings are saved.
- **Status:** `state.status.doorbell = {ok, at}`. After more than 2 min of network or 5xx failures, set `{error: "Doorbell alerts: can't reach Google (retrying)"}`.
- **Visibility:** `start()` runs only while `document.visibilityState === 'visible'`. `main.js` calls `doorbell.stop()` when the page is hidden and `start()` when it becomes visible again.
  - A hidden Chrome tab then can't steal presses from the kiosk.
  - Hidden pages' timers are throttled anyway [https://developer.chrome.com/blog/timer-throttling-in-chrome-88].
  - Unacked presses stay in the subscription (10 min retention), so a press made while another app was in front still rings on return if it is under 60 s old.
- **Starting conditions:** only when `google.hasNest && google.hasPubsub && SUB_RE.test(doorbellSub)`.
- **Abort support (util.js):** in `fetchJSON`, add `if (opts.signal) { if (opts.signal.aborted) ctrl.abort(); else opts.signal.addEventListener('abort', () => ctrl.abort(), { once: true }); }`, so that `stop()` can cancel an in-flight pull. `sleep(ms, signal)` must also be cancellable.
- **Old backlog:** no `:seek` is needed. With 10-min retention and the 60 s staleness check, old presses are simply acked and dropped.

## B6. Filtering a message
```js
export const CHIME = 'sdm.devices.events.DoorbellChime.Chime';
export function decodeData(b64) {                         // atob alone mangles UTF-8 device names
  return JSON.parse(new TextDecoder().decode(Uint8Array.from(atob(b64), (c) => c.charCodeAt(0))));
}
export function parseChime(p, { projectId, publishTime, now = Date.now() }) {
  const name = p?.resourceUpdate?.name, ev = p?.resourceUpdate?.events?.[CHIME];
  if (!ev || typeof name !== 'string' || !name.startsWith(`enterprises/${projectId}/devices/`)) return null;
  const at = Date.parse(p.timestamp || publishTime);      // 9-digit fractions parse in V8/Chromium
  if (!Number.isFinite(at) || now - at > STALE_MS) return { stale: true };   // future (clock skew) is fine
  const key = ev.eventId ? `${name}|${ev.eventSessionId}|${ev.eventId}` : `${name}|${p.eventThreadId || ev.eventSessionId}`;
  return { device: name, key, at };
}
```
- **Which messages ring:** only `DoorbellChime.Chime` messages from this Device Access project. Undecodable messages are ignored.
- **Ignored, but still acked:** Motion, Person, ClipPreview, `resourceUpdate.traits` (thermostat changes) and `relationUpdate`.
- **Duplicates:** use Home Assistant's token, device + `eventSessionId` + `eventId` [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/home-assistant/core/dev/homeassistant/components/nest/event.py], falling back to `eventThreadId`.
  - One press arrives as STARTED, then UPDATED/ENDED messages that repeat the same Chime [google-nest-sdm 9.2.1 tests `test_event_session_clip_preview_issue`, which reproduces HA #86314 on a battery doorbell], and Pub/Sub may deliver a message more than once.
  - **Never dedupe on the top-level `eventId`.**
  - A second press has a new Chime `eventId`, so it rings again.
- **Timing:** act on the first sighting, whatever `eventThreadState` says. Waiting for ENDED adds about 20 s [https://github.com/home-assistant/core/issues/87234].
- **Seen keys:** a Map pruned after 15 min, mirrored to `sessionStorage` so a reload in the middle of a ring doesn't chime again.
- **Stale window:** 60 s, as specified. If the owner reports missed rings, raise it to 90 s, since latency sometimes reaches 20–30 s and a redelivery adds up to 10 s.

## B8. Chime and unlocking sound (chime.js)
- **Create one context at boot,** and only if `doorbellSub` is set: `new (window.AudioContext || window.webkitAudioContext)({ latencyHint: 'interactive' })`.
- **Expected starting state:**
  - WebView Kiosk, top-level page: `running` with no tap, even with the kiosk's gesture setting on. Blink demands a gesture for Web Audio only in cross-origin iframes [Chromium audio_context.cc `UpdateAutoplayRequirement`; android_webview/browser/aw_settings.cc].
  - Chrome installed app (WebAPK): `running` [autoplay_policy.cc `IsInWebAppScope`; chrome_content_browser_client.cc].
  - Chrome tab: `suspended` until a tap.
- **Unlock:** add capture-phase, passive listeners for `pointerup`, `touchend`, `click` and `keydown` that call `ctx.resume()` when `state !== 'running'`.
  - Not `pointerdown`: a touch `pointerdown` doesn't count as a user activation [https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/interaction.html#activation-triggering-input-event].
  - Set `unlocked = true` the first time `state === 'running'`.
  - Show the sound hint while `!unlocked`.
- **`ring()` returns a boolean:**
  - If the last chime was under 5 s ago, return `true` and do nothing.
  - If `state !== 'running'` (including Chrome 136's `interrupted`), `await Promise.race([ctx.resume(), sleep(500)])`. In a blocked page, `resume()` can stay pending rather than fail.
  - If it's still not running, return `false`.
  - Otherwise play the ding-dong:
    - E5 659.25 Hz, then C5 523.25 Hz 0.5 s later.
    - Each note is a sine wave plus a quiet sine at twice the frequency (gain ×0.25).
    - Gain envelope: 0 → 0.8 in 10 ms, then an exponential fade to 0.001 over 1.4 s.
    - Play the pair twice, 2 s apart.
  - Suspend 15 s after the chime; once unlocked, a later `resume()` is allowed without a gesture.
  - Return `true`.
- **`test()`:** the same as `ring()` without the 5 s limit.
- **What not to use:** `navigator.getAutoplayPolicy()`, which Chrome and WebView don't support [https://github.com/mdn/browser-compat-data/blob/main/api/Navigator.json]; `<audio>` as the main path, because WebView locks each media element until a tap after every load [autoplay_policy.cc].
- **Volume:** output uses the media channel, so it follows media volume [audio_manager_android.cc]. Under default Do Not Disturb, media still plays [AOSP android12 default_zen_mode_config.xml, `media="true"`].
- **No new files:** the chime is generated, so there's no audio file and no CSP `media-src` change.

**findings:**

1) HOW EVENTS ARE ENABLED TODAY (changed 23 Jan 2025)
- Google stopped hosting Pub/Sub topics for new Device Access projects on 23 Jan 2025. New projects must create the topic in the developer's OWN Google Cloud project and pay any Pub/Sub costs themselves. Search snippets from Google's release notes and subscribe-to-events pages say: "Projects created after January 2025 must self-host their Pub/Sub topics, and you will need to provide your own topic ID." (https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/release-notes ; https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/subscribe-to-events ; HA issue #136656, 27 Jan 2025: https://github.com/home-assistant/core/issues/136656)
- Old projects may still have a Google-owned topic named `projects/sdm-prod/topics/enterprise-<device-access-project-id>`. HA calls this "the old way, but works completely fine". HA's library hard-codes that format as SDM_MANAGED_TOPIC_FORMAT and still detects it. A project paid for in 2026 will NOT get one. (HA docs source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/home-assistant/home-assistant.io/current/source/_integrations/nest.markdown ; google-nest-sdm admin_client.py in https://pypi.org/project/google-nest-sdm/ 9.2.1)
- The topic must let Google publish to it: grant principal `sdm-publisher@googlegroups.com` the role "Pub/Sub Publisher" (roles/pubsub.publisher) ON THE TOPIC. HA's tip: "Grant the role on the topic itself (using the topic's Permissions tab), not on the project's IAM & Admin page." HA's code applies exactly this IAM policy: {"bindings":[{"members":["group:sdm-publisher@googlegroups.com"],"role":"roles/pubsub.publisher"}]}. (nest.markdown; google-nest-sdm admin_client.py DEFAULT_TOPIC_IAM_POLICY; Google snippet at https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/subscribe-to-events: "enter sdm-publisher@googlegroups.com in New Principals and select Pub/Sub Publisher as the role")
- Device Access Console: open the project. The "Pub/Sub topic" row shows disabled. Click ⋮ → "Enable events with PubSub topic", enter the FULL topic name `projects/<cloud-project-id>/topics/<topic-id>`, then click "Add & Validate". Validation fails if the publisher grant is missing. (nest.markdown steps 9-13; https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/project/modify snippet: "Click the more_vert icon for Pub/Sub topic and select Enable events with PubSub topic")
- Google's model is one topic per Device Access project, carrying events for all authorised devices and structures. Pub/Sub delivers to "a pull or push subscription to that topic". To start events the first time after creating the subscription: "make a devices.list API call as a one-time trigger. Events for all structures and devices will publish after this call." Events keep arriving "as long as the access token is not revoked by the user and the event messages have not expired." (https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/subscribe-to-events snippets)
- Recent doc churn: HA revised its Nest GCP steps on 18 Jul 2026 (PR #46355: topic → Permissions tab → Add principal; the console button now says "Save", not "Create"). HA also rewrote its OAuth consent steps on 25 May 2026 for the new Google Auth Platform UI (Branding / Audience / Publish app). (https://github.com/home-assistant/home-assistant.io/commits/current/source/_integrations/nest.markdown ; https://github.com/home-assistant/home-assistant.io/pull/46355). Google's Pub/Sub docs moved from cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/* to docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/* (the old URLs return 301).

2) PULL SUBSCRIPTION (in the owner's Cloud project)
- Google's documented console flow is Pub/Sub → Subscriptions → "Create subscription". Fields: Subscription ID; select the topic; "Delivery type" = Pull; "Message retention duration"; "Retain acknowledged messages" checkbox; "Expiration period" ("Expire after this many days of inactivity" or never expire); "Acknowledgement deadline"; then filter, exactly-once, ordering, dead lettering and retry policy. (https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/create-subscription via snippet)
- The topic-creation dialog has an "Add a default subscription" checkbox, on by default, which creates `<topic-id>-sub` with default settings. (https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/create-topic via snippet)
- Limits and defaults (https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/subscription-properties via snippet):
  - Ack deadline: default 10 s, min 10 s, max 600 s.
  - Message retention: default 7 days, min 10 minutes, max 31 days.
  - Expiration: default 31 days of inactivity, min 1 day; can be set to never expire.
- HA's troubleshooting section warns that subscriptions left at the default "will be deleted after 31 days of inactivity" and recommends editing the subscription "to not expire". (nest.markdown)
- HA itself creates the subscription with only {"topic": ...}, i.e. all defaults, through the Pub/Sub REST API using the user's OAuth token. (google-nest-sdm admin_client.create_subscription)
- Recommended settings for this panel:
  - Delivery type: Pull.
  - Message retention: 10 minutes (the minimum). A doorbell press older than a minute is useless, and a short retention keeps the backlog tiny when the tablet is off.
  - Retain acknowledged messages: off.
  - Expiration: Never expire.
  - Ack deadline: leave at 10 s (or up to 30 s). The page acks immediately; a short deadline means a message caught in a dropped HTTP request is redelivered within seconds.
  - Filter: none. Pub/Sub filters match message attributes only; SDM puts everything in the JSON body, and I found no documented SDM attributes.
  - Exactly-once delivery, message ordering and dead lettering: all off. Retry policy: "Retry immediately".
- Pub/Sub is at-least-once with no ordering guarantee. Google's events page says to use the `timestamp` field to reconcile order, and says to ack and purge messages regularly to avoid duplicate deliveries. (https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/api/events snippets)
- Each subscription load-balances: two panels pulling the same subscription split the messages between them. Use one subscription per screen (standard Pub/Sub semantics; see the pull docs linked from nest.markdown: https://cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/pull#pull-workflow).

5) EVENT PAYLOADS
- Message shape (Google events page snippets + HA/library fixtures):
  - Top level: eventId (UUID of the message), timestamp (RFC3339, can carry up to 9 fractional digits, e.g. "2024-11-26T09:24:40.751177096Z"; Chromium's Date.parse accepts that, which I tested), resourceUpdate {name: "enterprises/<device-access-project-id>/devices/<device-id>", events{…} and/or traits{…}}, userId (obfuscated user id), resourceGroup (array of affected resource names; library fixtures spell it "resourcegroup"), eventThreadId, eventThreadState.
  - Relation events instead carry relationUpdate {type CREATED|UPDATED|DELETED, subject, object}.
  - Trait-change events (e.g. thermostat) carry resourceUpdate.traits and no thread fields.
- eventThreadState values, per Google: "STARTED — The first event in an event thread; UPDATED — An event in an ongoing event thread; ENDED — The last event in an event thread, which may be a duplicate of the last UPDATED event, depending on the thread type." Google also says: "This thread grouping and timing logic is handled by Google and is subject to change at any time." DoorbellChime.Chime "supports updateable notifications". An event thread is distinct from an event session: eventSessionId ties the Chime to its ClipPreview/Motion/Person events. (https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/api/events ; https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/traits/device/doorbell-chime snippets)

- The signed-in account must have Pub/Sub rights on the subscription. The project Owner does; another household member's account would get a 403.

SETTINGS
- Store the subscription as the full path and validate it against ^projects/[^/]+/subscriptions/[^/]+$ (HA's regex).
- Warn if the project part equals the Device Access project ID; HA has a matching error, "wrong_project_id".

PULL LOOP (runs while the panel runs)
- Request:
  - POST https://pubsub.googleapis.com/v1/{subscription}:pull
  - Headers: Authorization: Bearer <access token>, Content-Type: application/json
  - Body: {"maxMessages": 50}. Omit returnImmediately (deprecated); by default the server long-polls for a bounded time.
  - CORS preflight is allowed for authorization,content-type from the github.io origin (tested).
- Response: {} when nothing arrived, or {"receivedMessages":[{"ackId":"…","message":{"data":"<base64>","messageId":"…","publishTime":"…"}}]}.
- Wrap each pull in an AbortController with a ~90 s timeout, then immediately issue the next pull.
- Errors:
  - Network error or 5xx: back off 10 s, ×1.5, max 10 min (HA's values).
  - 401: refresh the token once (Google.api already does this).
  - 403 insufficient scopes: tell the owner to sign in again and tick every box.
  - 403 SERVICE_DISABLED: turn on the Cloud Pub/Sub API.
  - Billing-disabled or closed-trial errors: tell the owner to upgrade billing.
  - 404 NOT_FOUND: the subscription is missing or expired; recreate it and set Never expire.
- Decode each message: base64 → Uint8Array → new TextDecoder('utf-8').decode → JSON.parse. atob alone mangles non-ASCII.
- Ack EVERY received message immediately, including ignored ones, or they are redelivered:
  - POST https://pubsub.googleapis.com/v1/{subscription}:acknowledge
  - Body: {"ackIds":[…]}
  - Ack before the slow UI work.

HANDLING A MESSAGE
- ev = msg.resourceUpdate?.events; name = msg.resourceUpdate?.name ('enterprises/<DA-project>/devices/<id>').
- chime = ev?.['sdm.devices.events.DoorbellChime.Chime']. If present and name is the configured doorbell (or any device with trait sdm.devices.traits.DoorbellChime):
  - key = name + '|' + chime.eventSessionId + '|' + chime.eventId.
  - If the key has been seen, ignore it. The same Chime is repeated in UPDATED and ENDED messages, and Pub/Sub is at-least-once. Keep seen keys ~15 min, in memory plus sessionStorage.
  - If Date.now() - Date.parse(msg.timestamp) > ~120 s, ignore the message as stale, e.g. from a backlog after the tablet was off. Allow for clock skew.
  - Act on the first sighting whatever the eventThreadState (normally STARTED). Do not wait for ENDED, which can come ~20 s later.
- Action on a ring:
  - Show a full-screen overlay stacked above the night-dim overlay (22:30-06:30) and above the screensaver.
  - Play the chime.
  - Start the existing LiveStream (GenerateWebRtcStream) for that device.
  - Close after ~60-120 s, on tap, or when the stream ends (battery devices cap at ~5 min).
  - A second press is a new eventId: re-chime, but don't stack overlays.
- Ignore by default:
  - sdm.devices.events.CameraMotion.Motion and sdm.devices.events.CameraPerson.Person. Could optionally be a setting, e.g. wake the screen on Person at the front door.
  - sdm.devices.events.CameraClipPreview.ClipPreview, which carries previewUrl: GET with Bearer token, 10-frame mp4. CORS for previewUrl is untested.
  - resourceUpdate.traits (thermostat changes; could be used to refresh the thermostat tile) and relationUpdate.

OTHER
- One subscription per screen: panels sharing a subscription split messages between them. For testing on a laptop, create a second subscription on the same topic.
- Chime audio: browsers block un-gestured audio. Unlock an AudioContext/HTMLAudioElement on the first tap after load, and prefer Web Audio with a pre-decoded buffer. I did not verify here whether WebView Kiosk lets media play without a user gesture.
- Rate limits: one GenerateWebRtcStream per ring is well within SDM command limits (5 QPM per device, per the existing research notes).

### 2. pubsub

**research:**

**topic:**

Pulling Nest doorbell events from Cloud Pub/Sub directly in the browser (CORS, OAuth scope, long-poll pull semantics, ack/stale handling, quota and cost, IAM, and whether there's any non-Pub/Sub route)

**findings:**

SHORT ANSWER
Yes. A static GitHub Pages page can pull Pub/Sub messages straight from the browser. It uses the REST unary Pull (POST https://pubsub.googleapis.com/v1/projects/{p}/subscriptions/{s}:pull) with the same OAuth Bearer token the panel already uses for SDM. Three things are needed: (a) add https://www.googleapis.com/auth/pubsub to the scopes the Partner Connection Manager (PCM) URL asks for, (b) the owner creates a topic, lets Device Access publish to it, and enables events, and (c) the owner creates a pull subscription, or the page creates one. For one tablet the cost is zero, as long as the loop never sends more than about 4 pulls a second. A floor of one pull every 1–2 s gives large headroom. There is no realistic way to get doorbell presses without Pub/Sub.

1) CORS: verified live today (2026-09-27) from the sandbox with Origin: https://anginsemilir.github.io
- Preflight: OPTIONS .../subscriptions/test-sub:pull with Access-Control-Request-Method POST and Access-Control-Request-Headers authorization,content-type returned HTTP 200 with:
  - access-control-allow-origin: https://anginsemilir.github.io (the origin is echoed back)
  - access-control-allow-methods: DELETE,GET,HEAD,OPTIONS,PATCH,POST,PUT
  - access-control-allow-headers: authorization,content-type
  - access-control-max-age: 3600
- :acknowledge, :modifyAckDeadline and :seek returned the same result, as did PUT on subscriptions/{s} (create), GET on subscriptions/{s} and GET on topics/{t}.
- Real POST to :pull:
  - With no auth: 403 PERMISSION_DENIED ("Method doesn't allow unregistered callers").
  - With a bogus Bearer token: 401 UNAUTHENTICATED, reason ACCESS_TOKEN_TYPE_UNSUPPORTED, metadata method google.pubsub.v1.Subscriber.Pull.
  - Both error responses carried access-control-allow-origin, and access-control-expose-headers included www-authenticate. So the page can read Google's error bodies too.
- SDM (smartdevicemanagement.googleapis.com) gives the same preflight answer, which is why the panel's SDM calls already work.
- Real-world REST pull clients using fetch/HTTP with a Bearer token:
  - hparadiz/camera-notif (C/libcurl, March 2026): a Nest event pull loop posting {"maxMessages":N} to :pull and then :acknowledge.
  - googleworkspace/google-chat-samples (Apps Script UrlFetchApp :pull).
  - danieldriessen/ioBroker.google-home-nest (Node, REST :pull for SDM events).
- StreamingPull, which Home Assistant and google_nest_sdm use, is gRPC-only. The REST discovery document lists only acknowledge, create, delete, detach, get, getIamPolicy, list, modifyAckDeadline, modifyPushConfig, patch, pull, seek, setIamPolicy and testIamPermissions for subscriptions. From a browser, unary REST Pull is the only receive path. Push subscriptions need a public HTTPS endpoint, which a static page does not have.

2) OAuth scope
- The Pub/Sub discovery document (revision 20260915) lists exactly two scopes for pull, acknowledge, modifyAckDeadline, seek and create:
  - https://www.googleapis.com/auth/pubsub ("View and manage Pub/Sub topics and subscriptions")
  - https://www.googleapis.com/auth/cloud-platform ("See, edit, configure, and delete your Google Cloud data…")
  Use the narrower pubsub scope.
- Requesting it through the Nest PCM URL together with sdm.service is standard practice:
  - Home Assistant core homeassistant/components/nest/const.py has OAUTH2_AUTHORIZE = "https://nestservices.google.com/partnerconnections/{project_id}/auth" and SDM_SCOPES = [".../auth/sdm.service", ".../auth/pubsub"]. HA's test_config_flow.py asserts the authorize URL "…scope=https://www.googleapis.com/auth/sdm.service+https://www.googleapis.com/auth/pubsub&access_type=offline&prompt=consent".
  - The same pattern appears in potmat/homebridge-google-nest-sdm ("Note the +https://www.googleapis.com/auth/pubsub on the end. This is so you will have access to events."), dkilgore90/google-sdm-api (Hubitat), allenporter/python-google-nest-sdm, gdaybrice/gognestcli and hparadiz/camera-notif.
- The panel already sends calendar.readonly and tasks through PCM the same way, so adding a fourth scope fits the existing design.
- Sensitivity: a third-party doc (Prismatic, via search snippet) says the Pub/Sub scopes are classed as sensitive. I could not open Google's own scope list (developers.google.com is blocked here). It doesn't matter for this owner: the app already asks for sensitive scopes (sdm.service, calendar, tasks) and is published "In production" unverified (docs/google.md step 1.7). The owner already clicks past "Google hasn't verified this app", refresh tokens don't expire after 7 days in production (HA docs troubleshooting; Google refresh-token expiry rules), and the unverified-app 100-user cap is irrelevant for one user. The scope is not in the restricted class (Gmail/Drive-type) as far as I can tell (see uncertainties).
- With granular consent, the pubsub permission can show up as its own checkbox that the user must tick. The token response's scope string shows whether it was granted.

3) Pull semantics (the discovery document is the current primary source)
- PullRequest.maxMessages: "Required… Must be a positive integer. The Pub/Sub system may return fewer than the number specified."
- PullRequest.returnImmediately is marked "deprecated": true: "If this field set to true, the system will respond immediately even if there are no messages… Otherwise, the system may wait (for a bounded amount of time) until at least one message is available, rather than returning no messages. Warning: setting this field to true is discouraged… We recommend that users do not set this field."
- So an omitted/false returnImmediately gives a long poll whose server-side hold time is not documented ("bounded amount of time").
- Google's own client configs give a 60 s Pull deadline:
  - googleapis/google/pubsub/v1/pubsub_grpc_service_config.json: Pull timeout "60s", retry on UNKNOWN/ABORTED/UNAVAILABLE/INTERNAL.
  - python-pubsub subscriber transports/base.py: pull default_timeout=60.0.
  - A search snippet (Spring Cloud GCP docs) mentions 90 s client timeouts.
- Google's pull-troubleshooting page (search snippet) says a unary Pull response "with zero messages or with an error does not necessarily indicate that there are no messages available". Clients must simply loop.
- An empty result is the JSON {} (no receivedMessages key). A non-empty result is {"receivedMessages":[{"ackId","message":{"data":<base64>,"messageId","publishTime","attributes"?},"deliveryAttempt"}]}.
- maxMessages: doorbell presses are rare, but thermostat trait updates and motion events share the topic. 10 is a sensible value (Google examples use 10–20; camera-notif defaults to 10).
- Ack: POST :acknowledge {"ackIds":[...]}. "Acknowledging a message more than once will not result in an error"; acking after the deadline "may succeed, but such a message may be redelivered later". Subscription ackDeadlineSeconds defaults to 10 s (min 10, max 600).
- ModifyAckDeadline {ackIds, ackDeadlineSeconds 0–600}. 0 means nack (redeliver soon). The panel doesn't need it; ack right away instead.
- Delivery is at-least-once, so duplicates are possible. Pub/Sub does not keep order without ordering keys, so dedupe and use timestamps.

4) Page closed or restarted, and stale presses
- Unacked messages stay in the subscription for messageRetentionDuration: "Defaults to 7 days. Cannot be more than 31 days or less than 10 minutes." When the page reopens, the next pulls return the backlog: old chimes plus every thermostat or motion update.
- Three defences, all recommended:
  - (a) Create the subscription with messageRetentionDuration "600s" (the 10-minute minimum).
  - (b) On page start, POST :seek {"time": <now ISO>}. Per the discovery document: "Messages retained in the subscription that were published before this time are marked as acknowledged". This clears the backlog in one call.
  - (c) Ignore any DoorbellChime whose payload "timestamp" (fall back to message.publishTime) is more than about 60 s older than Date.now(), and dedupe by eventSessionId. One battery-doorbell press produces several messages (eventThreadState STARTED/UPDATED/ENDED, the same eventThreadId and eventSessionId, and a CameraClipPreview in the same session).
- google_nest_sdm merges events by eventSessionId and notifies once per session. Its EVENT_IMAGE_EXPIRE_SECS = 30 and CAMERA_CLIP_PREVIEW_EXPIRE_SECS = 15*60.
- HA issue #87234 shows the chime is present from the STARTED message: HA fired on ENDED and got about a 20 s delay. The HA docs now say event entities "are updated immediately when an event message is received without waiting for any media". So react to the first chime message and don't wait for ENDED.
- Subscription expiry: if expirationPolicy is unset, "a default policy with ttl of 31 days will be used"; "If expiration_policy is set, but expiration_policy.ttl is not set, the subscription never expires"; the minimum ttl is 1 day. Pulling counts as activity. The HA docs warn that subscriptions vanish after 31 days of inactivity and suggest "never expire".

5) Payload shape for a doorbell press (from google_nest_sdm tests and the SDM Events docs via search snippet)
{"eventId":"…","timestamp":"2026-…Z","resourceUpdate":{"name":"enterprises/<DA-project>/devices/<id>","events":{"sdm.devices.events.DoorbellChime.Chime":{"eventSessionId":"…","eventId":"…"}}},"userId":"…","eventThreadId":"…","eventThreadState":"STARTED","resourceGroup":["enterprises/…/devices/<id>"]}
- resourceUpdate.name uses the same full device name the panel already stores as cameraId (nest.js uses `${SDM}/${id}`).
- Thermostat updates arrive as resourceUpdate.traits. The panel could optionally use them for live thermostat refresh.
- What gets published depends on Google Home app notification settings. Per the HA docs: "Notifications: Push — Required for any detection event to be published"; "Away-Only — Events will only be published when a user is detected as away". The PCM consent also has a per-device "…know when there's a doorbell event" permission.

6) Cost and quota (Pub/Sub pricing page fetched today)
- "Every calendar month, the first 10 GiB of throughput identified as the Message Delivery Basic SKU for a billing account is free. After that, the price is $40 per TiB."
- "A minimum of 1 KB is assessed for each request, independent of the message sizes in the request."
- Treat every pull, including empty ones, as 1000 billable bytes. 10 GiB / 1000 B ≈ 10.7 M requests a month ≈ 4 requests/s sustained over a 31-day month. Worst cases:
  - 1 pull/s = 2.59 M requests ≈ 2.4 GiB a month.
  - 1 pull per 2 s ≈ 1.2 GiB.
  - Normal long-polling (tens of seconds per empty pull) is well under 0.1 GiB.
  - SDM's own publishes to the topic are billed to the topic's project ("The project that is billed for publishing is the project that contains the topic"). Thermostat/camera traffic is tiny.
- Storage: $0.27/GiB-month applies only to retained-acked messages, topic retention, snapshots, or "unacknowledged messages older than 1 day" when retention is over 1 day. That has been in effect since 30 June 2024 (search snippet; HA community thread). With 10-minute retention it never applies.
- Data transfer: "fees for internet data transfer… are consistent with the VPC network rates". Pull responses are a few hundred bytes, so at most cents a month even at 1 pull/s. Whether it's charged at all is unverified.
- Quotas: pull subscriber throughput is 240,000,000 kB/min per large region, measured in 1 kB units with a 1 kB minimum per request (quotas page via search snippet). One tablet is nowhere near it. Admin operations (create/seek) are also rate-limited for user accounts, but a few calls a day is fine.
- Billing: Pub/Sub requires billing enabled on the project (Pub/Sub quickstarts: "Make sure that billing is enabled"). The Cloud free trial ends at $300 spent or 90 days: "When your trial ends, your workloads get shut down… If you do not upgrade… by the end of the 30-day grace period, your workloads get deleted" (cloud.google.com/signup-faqs). After upgrading, "you will only be billed for usage beyond the $300 credits and free tier usage". The Free Tier page lists "Pub/Sub… 10 GB messages per month". The owner must click Upgrade before the trial ends, or doorbell alerts stop.

7) IAM
- Pull, ack, modifyAckDeadline and seek need pubsub.subscriptions.consume (roles/pubsub.subscriber). Creating a subscription also needs pubsub.subscriptions.create and pubsub.topics.attachSubscription.
- Basic roles/owner includes every permission, so an Owner signing in with a token carrying the pubsub scope can do all of this. Both the IAM role and the OAuth scope are required.
- The Cloud Pub/Sub API must be enabled in the project that owns the OAuth client (the quota/consumer project for user-token calls); here that's the same project. The panel's explainGoogleError already handles SERVICE_DISABLED text.
- The topic must grant roles/pubsub.publisher to group:sdm-publisher@googlegroups.com on the topic itself (google_nest_sdm DEFAULT_TOPIC_IAM_POLICY; HA docs: "Grant the role on the topic itself… not on the project's IAM & Admin page").
- One subscription feeds one consumer. Several clients on the same subscription are load-balanced, so each message goes to only one of them (Pub/Sub basics, via search snippet). The Chrome tab and the WebView Kiosk (or HA) must not share one subscription, or each sees only some presses.

8) What changed recently
- 23 Jan 2025: Device Access stopped hosting Pub/Sub topics for new projects. You create the topic in your own Cloud project, grant sdm-publisher@googlegroups.com Pub/Sub Publisher, then use "Enable events with PubSub topic" (HA docs; Device Access subscribe-to-events page via snippet). Older projects may still point at projects/sdm-prod/topics/enterprise-<id>.
- returnImmediately is formally deprecated (discovery revision 20260915).
- Google Cloud docs moved to docs.cloud.google.com (cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/* now returns 301).
- June 2024: unacked messages older than 1 day are billed.
- Pub/Sub Lite was turned down on 18 Mar 2026 (irrelevant here).
- The Nest Doorbell (wired, 3rd gen, 2025) exposes DoorbellChime but Google doesn't publish Chime events. Google issue 468317741 has been open since Dec 2025, reset to New in July 2026 (HA #177196, opened 24 Jul 2026). The Doorbell (battery) and the wired 1st/2nd gen are not reported as affected.

9) No way to do this without Pub/Sub
- The SDM DoorbellChime trait has no fields and no commands; presses exist only as sdm.devices.events.DoorbellChime.Chime Pub/Sub messages (DoorbellChime trait doc via search snippet). Polling SDM devices.get can never show a press, and ClipPreview is likewise event-only.
- Other routes:
  - Pub/Sub push, or a Cloud Run function relaying to FCM or web push. This needs server code, and Android WebView lacks the Push API (not verified).
  - The Google Home app's own notification on the tablet. It is a notification, not the panel's full-screen view. Full-screen doorbell live view is only for the Pixel Tablet in Hub Mode (Pixel Tablet Help).
  - The Home APIs (Android SDK), which need a native app.
- For this static page, REST Pull is the only option.

**implementation notes:**

Scope and sign-in (web/js/google.js)
- Add export const SCOPE_PUBSUB = 'https://www.googleapis.com/auth/pubsub'.
- In authUrl(), include it with SCOPE_SDM when g.projectId is set, and ideally only when doorbell alerts are configured: scope = 'sdm.service pubsub calendar.readonly tasks', space-separated, same PCM base URL, access_type=offline, prompt=consent.

- missingScopes() should list 'doorbell alerts' when events are configured but the scope is missing.
- Add to APIS in explainGoogleError: { host: 'pubsub.googleapis.com', service: 'pubsub.googleapis.com', title: 'Cloud Pub/Sub API', what: 'doorbell alerts' }. That gives SERVICE_DISABLED and ACCESS_TOKEN_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT plain-English messages.
- Also map 403 with reason BILLING_DISABLED (likely after the trial ends; not verified) to "Upgrade billing in Google Cloud".
- web/test/unit/google-nest.test.js and the mocks' scope strings will need updating.

Timeouts (web/js/util.js)
- fetchJSON aborts every request after 20 s by default, and Google.api() can't override it.
- Add a timeout pass-through, e.g. api(url, opts, timeoutMs), and use about 90 000 ms for :pull. Otherwise each long poll is killed at 20 s.
- If a pull is aborted after the server has put messages in it, those messages stay outstanding until the ack deadline (10 s) and are then redelivered. That's safe but adds up to 10 s delay.
- Treat AbortError on a pull as "empty; pull again now".
- The service worker (web/sw.js) ignores non-GET and cross-origin requests, so it won't interfere.

Endpoints (base const PS = 'https://pubsub.googleapis.com/v1'). All take Authorization: Bearer <access token>; bodies are JSON with Content-Type: application/json. The preflight is allowed and cached for 3600 s.
- Pull: POST `${PS}/${sub}:pull` body {"maxMessages":10}. Never send returnImmediately. Response is {} or {"receivedMessages":[{ackId, message:{data(base64), messageId, publishTime, attributes?}, deliveryAttempt}]}.
- Ack: POST `${PS}/${sub}:acknowledge` body {"ackIds":[...]} → {}.
- Seek, to drop the backlog on page start: POST `${PS}/${sub}:seek` body {"time": new Date().toISOString()} → {}.
- Optional self-provisioned subscription, one per panel install: PUT `${PS}/projects/${cloudProject}/subscriptions/panel-${randomId}` body {"topic":"projects/<p>/topics/nest-events","ackDeadlineSeconds":10,"messageRetentionDuration":"600s","expirationPolicy":{"ttl":"86400s"}}.
  - The 1-day TTL means an abandoned install's subscription deletes itself.
  - Use "expirationPolicy":{} for never-expire. Per the discovery doc, a policy with no ttl never expires (not live-tested).
  - 409 ALREADY_EXISTS is fine.
  - If :pull returns 404 NOT_FOUND, recreate and continue.
  - Store the name in settings.
- Validate names with /^projects\/[^\/]+\/subscriptions\/[^\/]+$/ and /^projects\/[^\/]+\/topics\/[^\/]+$/ (the same checks google_nest_sdm uses).

Loop (one pull in flight at a time, never parallel)
- Start: seek(now), then loop.
- Each iteration:
  1. t0 = Date.now().
  2. r = await pull(timeout 90 s).
  3. msgs = r?.receivedMessages || [].
  4. If msgs.length: handle each message synchronously first (show the doorbell right away), then fire-and-forget one :acknowledge with all ackIds. Ack every message, chime or not.
  5. If the response was empty and came back in under 2000 ms, sleep until 2 s have passed since t0. This caps billed empty pulls at 0.5/s, about 1.2 GiB a month worst case, under the 10 GiB free tier. Otherwise loop immediately.
- On HTTP 429/5xx/network error: back off 1 s → 2 → 4 … up to a 60 s cap, and reset after a success.
- On 401: api() already refreshes the token once.
- Show a small "doorbell alerts offline" indicator if the loop has been failing for more than about 2 minutes.

Handling a message
- Decode: p = JSON.parse(new TextDecoder().decode(Uint8Array.from(atob(m.message.data), c => c.charCodeAt(0)))).
- ev = p.resourceUpdate?.events?.['sdm.devices.events.DoorbellChime.Chime']. If !ev, ignore; optionally apply p.resourceUpdate.traits to the thermostat tile.
- Stale check: ts = Date.parse(p.timestamp || m.message.publishTime). Ignore if isNaN(ts) or Date.now() - ts > 60000. Negative values from clock skew are fine.
- Dedupe: key = ev.eventSessionId || p.eventThreadId || p.eventId. If it's in a Set of recent keys (keep the last ~50), ignore. This covers the STARTED/UPDATED/ENDED thread messages and at-least-once redelivery.
- Device: p.resourceUpdate.name. It equals the stored cameraId format "enterprises/<DA project>/devices/<id>". Open that device's live view, or the configured camera if it matches.
- Trigger on the first chime message (STARTED). Don't wait for ENDED; waiting costs about 20 s (HA #87234).
- The doorbell overlay must sit above the 22:30–06:30 dim overlay.
- For battery doorbells, keep the existing 5-minute stream cap.

SUMMARY
- In WebView Kiosk, Web Audio (AudioContext) in the top-level page can already play without any tap, whatever the kiosk's gesture setting. `<audio>.play()` and unmuted video still need either a tap or the kiosk setting "Media Playback Requires User Gesture" turned OFF.

(1) AUTOPLAY. Checked in the Chromium source (main branch, Sept 2026) and in the WebView Kiosk source (v0.26.21, 23 Sep 2026).

a) Android WebView (WebView Kiosk):
- The `WebSettings.setMediaPlaybackRequiresUserGesture` default is true (Android API reference).
- WebView maps that setting to Blink's autoplay policy: true gives `kUserGestureRequired`, false gives `kNoUserGestureRequired` (android_webview/browser/aw_settings.cc, ~line 700).
- Web Audio under `kUserGestureRequired`: `AudioContext::UpdateAutoplayRequirement()` sets `user_gesture_required_ = true` only when the frame is cross-origin to the outermost main frame. The field defaults to false (audio_context.h:487). So a top-level page's `new AudioContext()` starts 'running', and `resume()` or `start()` work with no gesture, even with the kiosk setting left ON (third_party/blink/renderer/modules/webaudio/audio_context.cc, ~lines 1468-1488 and 1491-1530).
- Media elements under `kUserGestureRequired` carry a per-element "gesture lock". `play()` without transient activation is rejected until that same element has been played or loaded inside a user gesture. After that the element stays unlocked (autoplay_policy.cc `ComputeLockPendingUserGestureRequired`, `RequestPlay`, `TryUnlockingUserGesture`; chromium docs/media/autoplay.md: "To unlock the gesture lock... call play() or load() on the element when responding to a user gesture"). Muted video is exempt, which is why the panel's muted WebRTC `<video>` already autoplays.
- WebView Kiosk does expose the setting:
  - UI: Settings → Web Engine → "Media Playback Requires User Gesture" (string `web_engine_media_playback_requires_user_gesture_title`). Default true (UserSettings.kt `mediaPlaybackRequiresUserGesture ... true`).
  - It is applied in utils/createCustomWebview.kt: `mediaPlaybackRequiresUserGesture = userSettings.mediaPlaybackRequiresUserGesture`.
  - Documented in docs/settings/web-engine.mdx section 14. Added in changelog 51 ("feat: added Media Playback Requires User Gesture under web engine", release 0.22.x, about Oct 2025).
  - Turning it OFF makes `<audio>.play()` and unmuted `<video>` play with no tap too.
  - It can also be set by Android managed configuration (app_restrictions.xml).

b) Chrome on Android (not WebView):
- Default policy is `kDocumentUserActivationRequired`. Sound is allowed once the frame has sticky activation (any activating tap since load), or `HadStickyUserActivationBeforeNavigation` (autoplay_policy.cc `IsDocumentAllowedToPlay`).
- Sticky activation carries over renderer-initiated navigations to the same host, so JS `location.reload()` keeps it (content/browser/renderer_host/navigation_request.cc: `was_activated` plus `ShouldPropagateUserActivation`, same host). A cold start (app restart, tablet reboot, OAuth round trip through accounts.google.com) needs a new tap.
- Installed app: `AutoplayPolicy::GetAutoplayPolicyForDocument` returns `kNoUserGestureRequired` when `document.IsInWebAppScope()`. On Android, `web_app_scope` is set from the tab delegate's `GetManifestScope()` (chrome_content_browser_client.cc ~line 4808). That returns the WebAPK's manifest scope only when the activity is a WebAPK activity (CustomTabDelegateFactory.java `getWebApkScopeUrl`: `if (!intentDataProvider.isWebApkActivity()) return null`).
- So a panel installed with Chrome "Install app" (a WebAPK) and opened from its icon plays sound with no tap ever. A plain browser-badged shortcut, or the page in a normal tab, does not get this.
- Chrome's public statement is the same: "Autoplay with sound is allowed if... The user has added the site to their home screen on mobile" (developer.chrome.com/blog/autoplay; source file updated 2021-05-25).
- Chromium also has `kAllowUnmutedAutoplayForTWA`, enabled by default, whose comment reads "for parity with PWAs" (chrome/browser/browser_features.cc).
- A developer report agrees: GoogleChromeLabs/bubblewrap issue #493 ("Sound won't autoplay at launch of Android app, but does with same code in installed PWA").
- WebAPK versus shortcut: only Chrome on devices with Google Mobile Services mints WebAPKs. They show up in the app drawer and in Android Settings → Apps; shortcuts don't (mdn/content PR #45530; web.dev/learn/pwa/installation).
- Other exemptions in the current source:
  - A site with camera or microphone permission set to Allow gets `kNoUserGestureRequired`. This is `media::kAutoplayBypassForMicCamera`, FEATURE_ENABLED_BY_DEFAULT in media/base/media_switches.cc; the code comment reads "received-video-call rings occurring before the user interacted".
  - An enterprise `AutoplayAllowlist` (kAutoplayPoliciesAndroid is enabled).
  - The Media Engagement Index applies only on desktop, per the Chrome blog.

c) Web Audio specifics (Chrome blog):
- "If an AudioContext is created before the document receives a user gesture, it will be created in the 'suspended' state, and you will need to call resume() after the user gesture."
- Detect this with `ctx.state` and `statechange`.
- In Blink, a suspended context unlocks at construction, `resume()` or source `start()` if sticky activation exists (`MaybeAllowAutoplayWithUnlockType` with kContextConstructor, kContextResume, kSourceNodeStart). After that `user_gesture_required_` stays false for the life of the document. `suspend()` then a later `resume()` without a gesture works.
- New since 2025: AudioContextState 'interrupted' shipped in Chrome 136, and applies to Chrome Android and WebView too (MDN BCD `BaseAudioContext.state.interrupted`: chrome 136, mirrored to Android and WebView; blink-dev Intent to Ship). In that state `resume()` rejects. Blink enters it when the frame is hidden only if the `media-playback-while-not-visible` permissions policy is disabled (it is allowed by default), or on an OS audio interruption.
- `navigator.getAutoplayPolicy()` is not supported in Chrome, Chrome Android or WebView (MDN BCD: Firefox 112+ only), so use `ctx.state`.
- User activation on a touch screen: the HTML spec's "activation triggering input events" are keydown, mousedown, pointerdown only when pointerType is "mouse", pointerup when pointerType is not "mouse", and touchend. A touch `pointerdown` does NOT grant activation. Unlock on pointerup, touchend or click. The panel's current lastTouch listener uses pointerdown, which is fine for its own purpose but wrong for unlocking audio.
- The Fullscreen API (`requestFullscreen`) needs transient activation (MDN), so the doorbell view must be a CSS full-viewport overlay, not `requestFullscreen()`.
- Audio route: Chromium's Android output uses `AAUDIO_USAGE_MEDIA` (media/audio/android/audio_manager_android.cc), so the chime follows the MEDIA volume, not ring or notification volume.

(2) THROTTLING, SCREEN OFF, OTHER APP IN FRONT

Visible page:
- Chrome's rules put a visible page in "minimal throttling": timers aren't throttled beyond the 4 ms clamp for nested chains. Fetch and WebSocket are unaffected.
- Hidden page: timers are checked once per second. After more than 5 minutes hidden, silent for 30 s, with no WebRTC and a chain count of 5 or more, they run once per MINUTE ("intensive throttling", developer.chrome.com/blog/timer-throttling-in-chrome-88).
- Browsers may also freeze or discard hidden pages (developer.chrome.com Page Lifecycle API). Chrome 133 (Feb 2025) added freezing on Energy Saver on desktop (developer.chrome.com/blog/freezing-on-energy-saver).

In WebView:
- `WebView.onPause()` "does not pause JavaScript"; only `pauseTimers()` does (Android WebView reference).
- WebView Kiosk never calls `webView.onPause()`, `pauseTimers()` or `resumeTimers()` (grep of its source).
- So while the kiosk is in front with the screen on, nothing is throttled. With the screen off or another app in front, the page is hidden and Blink's hidden-page throttling applies (expected behavior, not device-tested).

Doze:
- It only starts when the device is "unplugged and stationary... with the screen off" (developer.android.com doze-standby, updated 2026-08-18). An always-charging tablet doesn't enter Doze.

Keep-screen-on scope:
- WebView Kiosk's "Keep Screen On" sets FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON only while its activity is resumed: `ON_PAUSE -> setKeepScreenOn(false)` in ui/components/webview/KeepScreenOn.kt. Settings → Device → Keep Screen On, default false.
- The Screen Wake Lock API applies to "visible (active) documents" and is released when the document is not visible (MDN Screen Wake Lock API).
- So when the panel opens Spotify, Claude or Gemini, Android's screen timeout applies.
- The "Stay awake" developer option "Sets your screen to stay on while the device is plugged in" whatever app is in front (developer.android.com/studio/debug/dev-options, updated 2026-09-16).

Waking the screen or coming to the front:
- There is no web API to turn the screen on or bring a page to the front. Wake Lock only prevents dimming or locking for a visible document. Fullscreen needs a user gesture.
- Android 10+ blocks background activity starts unless, for example, the app holds SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW or the launch comes from a notification tap's PendingIntent (developer.android.com background activity starts).
- MacroDroid can do it:
  - "Notification" trigger (Notification Received, filter by app and text).
  - "Screen On" action ("wakes the screen up"; "Screen On (Alternative)" for devices where the standard option doesn't work).
  - "Launch Application" action ("On Android 10+ MacroDroid requires the 'Display over other apps' permission to launch apps from the background").
  - "Send Intent" action (Action android.intent.action.VIEW, Package, Data URL).
- WebView Kiosk has its own wake path:
  - MQTT command payloads accept `"wakeScreen": true` (docs/settings/mqtt/topics/command.mdx). It requires Settings → MQTT → Use Foreground Service, and battery optimisation disabled. Implementation: FULL_WAKE_LOCK | ACQUIRE_CAUSES_WAKEUP, modelled on the Home Assistant COMMAND_SCREEN_ON code.
  - UnifiedPush (ntfy or sunup distributor) accepts the same commands (go_to_url, refresh, notify, launch_package...). With ntfy and "Process Unencrypted Messages", a plain `curl -d '{"type":"command","command":"...","wakeScreen":true}' https://ntfy.sh/<endpoint>?up=1` works (docs/settings/unifiedpush/integration.mdx).
  - These need something server-side to send the message on a doorbell press, so they fall outside a static-page-only setup.
  - Recent: MQTT wakeScreen in changelog 107 (0.25.2, 22 Dec 2025); UnifiedPush in changelog 114 (0.26.0, 24 Jan 2026).

**implementation notes:**

Chime module (new file, e.g. web/js/chime.js; no server needed):

1. At boot, create ONE AudioContext:
   ```
   const AC = window.AudioContext || window.webkitAudioContext;
   const ctx = new AC({ latencyHint: 'interactive' });
   ```
   - Preload the chime: `fetch('sounds/doorbell.mp3')` → `arrayBuffer()` → `ctx.decodeAudioData()`. decodeAudioData works while the context is suspended. Cache the file in sw.js.
   - Or synthesize a two-tone ding-dong with OscillatorNode + GainNode envelopes, so no asset is needed.
   - Expected `ctx.state` right after creation:
     - WebView Kiosk: 'running' (top frame, any setting)
     - Chrome WebAPK opened from its icon: 'running'
     - Chrome tab after a JS reload that followed a tap: 'running'
     - Otherwise: 'suspended'
2. Unlock on any touch. Add capture-phase, passive listeners on document for 'pointerup', 'touchend', 'click' and 'keydown'. NOT 'pointerdown' alone: a touch pointerdown doesn't grant user activation under the HTML spec. The handler:
   - calls `ctx.resume()` if `ctx.state !== 'running'`;
   - once, calls `play()` then `pause()` on a single reusable muted `HTMLAudioElement` (`el.muted = true; el.play().then(() => { el.pause(); el.currentTime = 0; el.muted = false; })`). This unlocks that element's per-element gesture lock in WebView when "Media Playback Requires User Gesture" is still ON.
   - Leave the listeners installed (cheap). Also listen to `ctx.onstatechange` to update a status flag.
3. ring(opts):
   - If state isn't 'running', `await ctx.resume().catch(() => {})`.
   - If it's now 'running', play the buffer via `BufferSource → Gain(volume) → destination`. Repeat 2x with a gap. Use a lower gain in night hours (`settings.panel.nightFrom/nightTo`).
   - Else fall back to `el.play()` of the unlocked `<audio>` element.
   - If that rejects (NotAllowedError), show a big visual flash and a banner "Tap for sound", and return false.
   - Treat 'interrupted' (Chrome 136+) like 'suspended': retry resume on the next attempt, and don't throw.
   - Optionally `ctx.suspend()` about 15 s after the chime to avoid keeping the audio device open 24/7. A later `resume()` without a gesture is allowed once the context has been unlocked, per Blink `user_gesture_required_`.
   - Don't use `navigator.getAutoplayPolicy()`: Chrome and WebView don't support it.
4. Doorbell flow in main.js:
   - On a new doorbell event, de-duplicate by eventId and ignore events older than about 60-90 s. Then:
     - set `nightSnoozeUntil` or call `updateNight()` so the dim overlay hides (`updateNight` already hides it when `live` is set);
     - open the doorbell live view expanded full-viewport through the existing `openCamera()` path, as a CSS overlay. Do NOT call `element.requestFullscreen()`: it needs transient activation and will fail without a tap;
     - call `ring()`.
   - If `window.WebviewKioskBrightnessInterface` exists, `setBrightness(100)` (or -1 for system) while the doorbell view is open, and restore the previous `getBrightness()` value afterwards.
   - Auto-close after the battery doorbell's roughly 5-minute stream cap.
   - Keep the WebRTC `<video>` muted (it autoplays everywhere). Offer an "Unmute" button: the tap is a gesture, so it works in every runtime.
5. Catch-up when the panel comes back to the front: on `visibilitychange` → 'visible' (and on `pageshow`), poll for doorbell events at once and, if a press happened within the last ~60 s, run the doorbell flow. A hidden page's timers are throttled to 1/s, then 1/min after 5 minutes hidden (Chrome timer-throttling rules), so events can arrive late while another app is in front. MacroDroid bringing the kiosk forward then triggers the catch-up. A hidden page can still play Web Audio (the media-playback-while-not-visible policy is allowed by default), but don't rely on the page running while hidden.
6. Runtime detection, for Settings and checklist messages:
   - WebView: UA contains '; wv)' or `window.WebviewKioskBrightnessInterface`.
   - Installed Chrome app: `matchMedia('(display-mode: fullscreen)').matches || matchMedia('(display-mode: standalone)').matches`.
   - Otherwise a Chrome tab.
   - Show: "Doorbell sound: ready" (`ctx.state === 'running'`) / "Tap once to enable doorbell sound" (suspended) / "Sound blocked by the kiosk setting" (WebView `<audio>` fallback rejected). Add a "Test chime" button.
7. Wake Lock: keep the existing `navigator.wakeLock.request('screen')` and re-request on visibilitychange. It can't turn the screen on; it only keeps a visible page from dimming. The manifest already uses `display: fullscreen` and `scope: './'`, so the installed WebAPK scope covers the panel URL.
8. No new OAuth scopes or endpoints are needed for sound or display. How doorbell events reach the page (Pub/Sub pull, etc.) is a separate topic.

**corrections:**

- EVENT DE-DUPLICATION (implementation note 4 is wrong as written). The note says to 'de-duplicate by eventId'. A DoorbellChime.Chime is an 'updateable notification' event, so one press can arrive as several Pub/Sub messages. They share an eventThreadId, with eventThreadState STARTED, then UPDATED, then ENDED, and share an eventSessionId, but each message carries its own top-level eventId. Fix: ring and open the view only for the first message of a new eventThreadId (or eventSessionId) that contains sdm.devices.events.DoorbellChime.Chime, and ignore UPDATED/ENDED messages for a thread already handled. Otherwise one press can chime 2-3 times. Sources: https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/api/events and https://developers.google.com/nest/device-access/traits/device/doorbell-chime (search snippets: 'updated using the content of a subsequent event with the same eventThreadId'; 'STARTED indicates the first event in an event thread, UPDATED an event in an ongoing event thread'). python-google-nest-sdm groups messages by eventSessionId and waits for the thread to end: https://github.com/allenporter/python-google-nest-sdm/blob/main/google_nest_sdm/event_media.py (async_handle_events).
- GOOGLE HOME SETTINGS ALSO CONTROL WHETHER SDM PUBLISHES EVENTS (missing warning in B3/B6). B6 suggests silencing the Google Home alert so there aren't two alerts.
